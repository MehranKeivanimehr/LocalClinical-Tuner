# Data card - LocalClinical-Tuner clinical notes dataset

> Auto-generated by `data/quality_filter.py` / `scripts/prepare_dataset.py`. Do not edit by hand.

**Contains no real patient data.** Encounters are either (a) fully synthetic notes written by an LLM from sampled fictional scenarios, or (b) public, de-identified/role-played doctor-patient dialogues released under CC BY 4.0. Identifier patterns are scrubbed from public data and forbidden in synthetic data. Intended use: research on privacy-preserving clinical documentation models. **Not for clinical use.**

## Quality filtering (both sources)

Pipeline: global near-dedup -> rule-based clinical sanity checks -> independent LLM critic. Rules reject on any critical finding or > 1 major findings; the critic rejects on any critical error, overall < 3, or any dimension <= 2. ## Known limitations

- Synthetic notes reflect the teacher model's view of clinical practice and may under-represent rare presentations, documentation errors, and real-world messiness.
- Gold labels are model-generated (or reused references) and filtered automatically; they are not clinician-verified. Use the human-review sample to estimate residual error.
- Public dialogues are role-played (ACI-Bench) or derived from sample reports (MTS-Dialog); they are short or US-centric and English-only.
- When no API is available, public-data labels come from the local teacher and are criticized by the same model family that later serves as the evaluation judge (Qwen2.5-72B); it filtered, but did not write, any training data.
- Rule checks are high-precision heuristics (regex extraction, a small curated drug table) and will miss errors that the critic must catch; the critic itself is an LLM and can be wrong.
